# Privacy Policy - Par for the Course

**Developer:** Promethean Games LLC  
**Effective Date:** July 8, 2026  
**Contact:** info@promethean-games.com

Par for the Course is a golf scoring application. This policy explains what data is processed, why it is processed, and your choices.

## 1) Data We Process

Depending on features you use, we may process:

- **Gameplay data**: player names, scores, holes, settings, tournament records.
- **Device identifiers**: device-scoped identifier used for trial/unlock entitlement and anti-abuse controls.
- **Online tournament data** (if you use online sync): room codes, participant entries, and synced score events.
- **Billing verification metadata**: purchase token/transaction identifiers, product ID, package/bundle ID, purchase state, and entitlement status.
- **Basic technical diagnostics**: error logs and request metadata needed to operate and secure services.

We do **not** collect payment card numbers. Purchases are processed by platform billing providers.

## 2) How We Use Data

We use data to:

- Provide app features (score tracking, tournament leaderboard/sync).
- Verify and maintain purchase entitlement (trial + one-time unlock).
- Detect abuse/fraud and keep services reliable.
- Improve app stability and performance.

## 3) Billing Providers

On Android, in-app purchases are processed through **Google Play Billing**. Google handles payment card processing. We only receive/store metadata required to validate purchases and unlock access.

## 4) Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers required to operate the app (for example, infrastructure or billing verification workflows), and only as needed for functionality, security, and compliance.

## 5) Data Storage and Retention

- **On-device/local data**: stored on your device until you remove it or uninstall.
- **Server-side tournament/billing records**: retained only as long as needed for app functionality, fraud prevention, legal obligations, and support.
- We periodically remove or anonymize data no longer required.

## 6) Your Choices and Controls

- You can delete local app data using in-app controls (where available) or by uninstalling/reinstalling.
- You can request data access or deletion by emailing: **info@promethean-games.com**.

## 7) Children’s Privacy

Par for the Course is not directed to children under the age where parental consent is required by applicable law. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## 8) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect data. No method of transmission/storage is 100% secure.

## 9) International Processing

If you access the app from outside the service hosting region, your data may be processed in other countries where our providers operate.

## 10) Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date and, where required, providing additional notice.

## 11) Contact

Questions or privacy requests: **info@promethean-games.com**
